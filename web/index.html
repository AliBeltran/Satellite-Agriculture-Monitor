◇ ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Satellite Agriculture Monitor uses geospatial and satellite-observation technology to help visualize and analyze agricultural fields. It is designed to help producers, agronomists, conservation professionals, and others in agriculture explore field information and support better-informed decisions. The project is open source and is being developed to make agricultural technology more accessible to producers and the agricultural community.
